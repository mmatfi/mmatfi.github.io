Privacy Policy

Application Name: rclone Personal Client · Last Updated: November 25, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how rclone Personal Client ("the Application") accesses, uses, stores, and protects Google user data when connecting to Google services via the open-source rclone command-line utility.

1. Data Accessed

When you authorize the Application, it accesses only the Google account data covered by the OAuth scopes you explicitly grant—such as Google Drive files, folders, and metadata—solely to perform file transfers, synchronization, listing, and management operations requested by you in rclone.

2. How Data Is Used

Authorized Google data is transferred directly between Google's APIs and your local machine or server running rclone. The Application does not use Google user data for advertising, profiling, analytics, or any purpose other than executing your requested file operations.

3. Data Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell, rent, trade, or share your Google user data or OAuth tokens with any third parties. Data is not transmitted to any external servers operated by the Application developer.

4. Data Storage and Security

OAuth access and refresh tokens are stored locally in your rclone configuration file (typically rclone.conf) on the device where you run rclone. File contents and metadata are not persisted on any third-party cloud or developer-controlled server. You are responsible for securing local file permissions on your device and configuration files.

5. Data Retention and Revocation

Tokens remain in your local rclone.conf until you delete the remote entry or file, or until you revoke access. You can revoke the Application's access to your Google account at any time by visiting your Google Account Permissions page and removing the Application.
